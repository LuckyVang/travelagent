# 7-Day Switzerland Hiking & Alpine Itinerary (Jungfrau Region)

#### **Day 1: Arrival & Interlaken Orientation**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive in Switzerland (typically via Zurich or Geneva) and take the scenic train ride into **Interlaken**, nestled between Lakes Thun and Brienz.
*   **Afternoon Activity:** Settle in and take a gentle stroll around the town or along the Aare River to shake off travel fatigue.
*   **Refreshment Stop:** Grab a soothing cup of tea or hot chocolate at one of Interlaken's local bakeries.
*   **Evening:** Enjoy a relaxing dinner in town.

#### **Day 2: Eiger Trail & Kleine Scheidegg Hike**
*   **Morning:** Take the mountain train up toward the **Eigergletscher** station.
*   **Hiking:** Hike the famous **Eiger Trail**, which sits right beneath the massive north face of the Eiger. The trail offers breathtaking views down into the Grindelwald valley.
*   **Lunch / Break:** Stop by **Bergrestaurant Kleine Scheidegg** for a bite with panoramic mountain views.
*   **Afternoon:** Continue descending toward Kleine Scheidegg or take the cogwheel train back down, soaking in the glacier views (*Giessengletscher*, *Moench*, and *Jungfrau*).

#### **Day 3: Jungfraujoch – Top of Europe**
*   **Morning:** Take the high-altitude railway up to **Jungfraujoch - Top of Europe**, the highest railway station in Europe.
*   **Activities:** Explore the Sphinx Observatory viewing platform, walk through the Ice Palace, and view the massive Aletsch Glacier. 
*   **Lunch:** Dine at one of the mountain-top eateries such as **Restaurant Eiger** or **Restaurant Crystal**.
*   **Afternoon:** Descend gradually back down to the valley for a quieter evening in Grindelwald or Wengen.

#### **Day 4: Wengen & Valley Explorations**
*   **Morning:** Head to the car-free alpine village of **Wengen**. 
*   **Hiking:** Enjoy a mid-altitude ridge hike offering sweeping vistas across the Lauterbrunnen Valley (known for its dramatic cliffs and waterfalls).
*   **Afternoon:** Explore local artisan shops and relax at an alpine teahouse.

#### **Day 5: Lauterbrunnen Waterfall & Cliff Trails**
*   **Morning:** Visit the dramatic **Lauterbrunnen Valley** (the "Valley of 72 Waterfalls"). Hike the valley floor trail past cascading falls like Staubbach Fall.
*   **Afternoon:** Take the cable car up to Mürren for a cliffside walking trail (*Northface Trail*) that treats you to iconic skyline views of the Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau.

#### **Day 6: Panoramic Ridge Hike & Alpine Lakes**
*   **Morning/Afternoon:** Choose a high-altitude panoramic trail (such as the Mannlichen to Kleine Scheidegg trail or paths around First / Bachalpsee, depending on seasonal trail openings). These routes are world-class for hikers looking for wide-open alpine scenery.
*   **Evening:** Head back down to Interlaken or your base village for a celebratory final dinner.

#### **Day 7: Souvenirs & Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely final Swiss breakfast, perhaps with some local herbal tea or hot chocolate, and pick up any souvenirs.
*   **Afternoon:** Board your train back to the airport for your departure flight.
