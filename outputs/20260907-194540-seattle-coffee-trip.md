# 1-Day Seattle Coffee & Sights Itinerary (September 20, 2026)

*Note: Weather for your trip is forecasted to be pleasant with a high around 73°F and a low of 55°F.*

## Morning: Capitol Hill Coffee & Views
* **8:30 AM – 9:30 AM:** Start your day in Capitol Hill at **Victrola Coffee Roasters** (11th Ave). Enjoy a meticulously roasted pour-over or espresso in their spacious, industrial-chic loft cafe.
* **9:30 AM – 11:00 AM:** Take a stroll through **Volunteer Park**. Walk up the historic Water Tower for panoramic views of the Seattle skyline, Space Needle, and surrounding Sound.

## Midday: Downtown & Pike Place
* **11:30 AM – 1:00 PM:** Head downtown and grab lunch around **Pike Place Market**. While exploring the bustling stalls and fresh seafood vendors, stop by **Ghost Alley Espresso** tucked right beneath the market for a cozy, atmospheric coffee refill.
* **1:00 PM – 2:30 PM:** Walk down to the **Seattle Waterfront** and take a ride on the **Seattle Great Wheel** or walk along the newly revitalized Pier 62 for fresh sea breezes and views of Bainbridge Island.

## Afternoon: Pioneer Square & Specialty Roasters
* **3:00 PM – 4:30 PM:** Make your way down to historic **Pioneer Square**. Explore the charming 19th-century Romanesque Revival architecture, art galleries, and historic Occidental Square.
* **4:30 PM – 5:30 PM:** Wrap up your cafe tour at **Caffe Umbria** in Pioneer Square for a true Italian-style espresso experience to recharge for the evening.

## Evening: Dinner & Skyline Sunset
* **6:00 PM:** Enjoy dinner at one of Pioneer Square or downtown's fantastic farm-to-table restaurants (such as Taylor Shellfish Oyster Bar or Seattle-style seafood spots).
