# 5-Day Seattle Itinerary: Architecture, Culture & Scenic Exploration

*Note: Flight and hotel booking features are coming soon, so this itinerary focuses on the daily experience and activities. Additionally, OpenTripMap has limited coverage for specific outdoor hiking trails, so we've highlighted local landmarks and architectural points of interest within Seattle, with plenty of room for day trips to nearby hiking regions like the Cascades or Olympics! Also, since you prefer not to drink coffee, we've skipped the local cafe stops.*

---

### **Day 1: Arrival & Downtown Seattle Exploration**
- **Morning / Afternoon:** Arrive in Seattle, settle into your accommodation, and stretch your legs with a walk through downtown. 
- **Sightseeing:** Visit the striking **Central Library** to admire its unique architecture, and check out the historic **Rainier Club** and the **William Kenzo Nakamura United States Courthouse**.
- **Evening:** Enjoy dinner at a local eatery downtown (such as *Kigo Kitchen - Columbia Center*) and take in panoramic views from the **Sky View Observatory**.

### **Day 2: Day Hike Adventure (Pacific Northwest Trails)**
- **All Day:** Head out of the city for a dedicated hiking day! Depending on your preferred difficulty, explore nearby trails in the **Cascades** or **Snoqualmie Pass** region (such as Rattlesnake Ledge or Twin Falls for stunning mountain and waterfall views). 
- **Evening:** Return to Seattle to relax and enjoy a quiet dinner after a long day on the trails.

### **Day 3: Waterfront & Cultural Landmarks**
- **Morning:** Stroll along the Seattle waterfront and take in views of Puget Sound. 
- **Afternoon:** Explore local cultural spots, stopping by the **Three Piece Sculpture #3: Vertebrae** and historic downtown architecture. 
- **Evening:** Have a relaxed dinner near the waterfront.

### **Day 4: Second Hiking Day / Nature Exploration**
- **All Day:** Head toward the **Olympic Peninsula** or lower trails closer to the Sound for another immersive day of hiking and breathing in the crisp evergreen air. 
- **Evening:** Head back into town for a comfortable evening.

### **Day 5: City Views & Departure**
- **Morning:** Revisit your favorite downtown spots or catch any final sights you missed. 
- **Afternoon:** Wrap up your trip and depart for home.
